Unfortunately, current law obliges all website operators to ask users to approve the processing of their data. Hardly anyone reads the contents of these pages, and almost everyone, so as not to waste time, unconditionally approves any abuse and imposition in the use of their data (without such legislation this would not happen...).
The whole thing is rather comical and also somewhat absurd (especially given the massive commercialisation of personal data that goes on today), but there it is... so we too present to you the pointless content of our Privacy policy...!

The purpose of this page is to provide maximum transparency about the information the site collects and how it uses it.

In compliance with the obligations arising from European legislation (the European General Data Protection Regulation), we respect and protect the confidentiality of visitors and users, making every possible and proportionate effort not to infringe users' rights.
We do not publish advertisements; however, we may use third-party services in order to improve the use of the site, third parties that may collect users' data and then use it to send personalised advertisements on other sites. Nevertheless, we make every effort to protect users' privacy and to minimise the collection of personal data, for example:

  • we use YouTube videos set up so as not to send cookies (and therefore not to collect data) until the user clicks on the video (in place of the video there is only a placeholder);
  • we use social plugins to make it easier to share articles on social networks, but configured so that they send cookies (and therefore possibly collect data) only after the user has clicked on the plugin;
  • by default, we block data collection via Google FLoC.

For information purposes, please note that cookie management is complicated, both for us as site operators (we may not be able to manage third-party cookies correctly) and for the user (who might block cookies essential to the site's operation). A better and simpler way to protect privacy is to use anti-tracking tools, which then block tracking by third-party sites (read -> Online privacy protection).
To this end, we recommend installing suitable software for this purpose on your device(s).
